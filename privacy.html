VenTra X Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 13, 2026

Information We Collect

VenTra X is a paper trading simulator app. We collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Nickname you provide during registration (optional)
  • Trading Data: Buy/sell records and portfolio data from simulated trading
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version (for improved user experience)

How We Use Information

  • Provide paper trading functionality
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Analyze usage data to optimize our product

Data Storage

Your trading data is stored locally on your device or in the cloud (Firebase). We do not sell your data to any third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase: Cloud storage and authentication

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be notified through the app.
